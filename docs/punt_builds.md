# Punt builds: does conceding categories win here?

Full Court Press (ESPN 3853870), 2019-2026, 98 team-seasons. Chosen punts use preseason projections, so they are measured on the 72 team-seasons with usable projections (2019, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025, 2026). Generated by `scripts/punt_builds.py`.

## 1. Summary

- **Choosing a punt at the draft, against teams of the same projected strength:** category win rate -0.028 (95% CI -0.059 to +0.001) for the 42 teams with at least one chosen punt. Confidence: **suggestive**.
- **Two or more chosen punts:** -0.031 (95% CI -0.077 to +0.011), n=23. Confidence: **suggestive**.
- **A strict chosen punt** (a category 1.5 SD below the league at the draft): -0.041 (95% CI -0.059 to -0.024), n=23. Confidence: **strong**.
- **Specialization and matchups:** the most specialized tercile converts category win rate into matchup wins at -0.016 (95% CI -0.054 to +0.034) against the least specialized, within the same strength tercile. Confidence: **no evidence**.
- **Close weeks:** one-category weeks (5-4 or 4-5) won, most specialized minus least specialized of the same strength: -12% (95% CI -20% to -2%). Confidence: **strong**.
- **Playoffs:** teams with a chosen punt make the playoffs at -10% (95% CI -30% to +12%) against non-punters. Confidence: **suggestive**.
- **Reverse causality:** realized punts (categories lost most weeks) track weakness, not choice; every claim above is drawn from chosen punts or strength-matched comparisons, and section 2 shows how rarely a chosen punt stays a realized one.

## 11. What this means for a 15-team, nine-category draft

- **Do not draft to concede categories.** A roster built at the draft to be far behind the league in a category won fewer categories than rosters of the same projected strength, in the strict test with an interval clear of zero. The looser tests point the same way without clearing zero.
- **The bias runs in punting's favour, and it still loses.** A punted category drags a roster's projected strength down, which lowers what it is expected to win; punt rosters fell short even of that lowered expectation.
- **Specialized teams lose the close weeks.** Conceding categories leaves no margin: one-category weeks go against the most specialized teams, and H2H seasons are decided there.
- **Spend balanced and stay balanced.** Balanced spending with no chosen punt was the best cell in section 8; top-heavy spending with a punt was the worst.
- **Thin evidence:** build types, playoffs, titles and league size are all small cells and six seasons of projections; none of them should drive a decision on its own.

## 12. Traps

- Chosen punts rest on preseason projections, which are unusable for 2020 (no stats) and 2023 (a mid-season snapshot); those seasons count only in the realized and specialization analyses.
- A team in the bottom quartile of projected strength is never counted as choosing a punt: a weak roster projects bottom-two in many categories without a plan.
- Category rates here are regular-season matchups only, ties counted as half. Byes (no opponent) are excluded.
- The loose chosen-punt test (bottom two of the league) catches rosters that were merely thin somewhere; only about one chosen category in five went on to be lost most weeks. The strict test (1.5 SD below) is the one to read.
