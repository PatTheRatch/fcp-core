What it needs, and what it will not do

From you

An ESPN fantasy basketball league played head-to-head in the nine categories, and an email address to sign in with. No password, ever: the link is the sign-in. One member of the league connects it with his own ESPN login and sends everyone else an invite.

Not on your behalf

Nothing here touches ESPN on your behalf. No add, no drop, no claim, no trade, no lineup is ever submitted for you. It reads your league with a member's login and writes back nothing at all.
