Needs attention

    A line is a fact that asks for a decision today, and it is drawn only when a route already read here says so: a place in tonight's lineup the bench could fill (today's fix and benched), a starter ESPN has out or in doubt tonight (today's lineup), a day left in the period with a place going empty that the wire could fill (pickups/stream's empty_days), and the first move over the bar (pickups/stream's moves; the rest are The wire). Before the draft it is the draft. Nothing else, and when nothing asks, there is no section.

    Largest figure first. The figure is the number of starting places the line is about -- the places the bench could fill tonight, one for a starter out or in doubt, the places going empty that day -- or, for a move, its net in categories. A tie keeps the order the kinds are listed in above.

    Facts with their figures, and a link to where each is acted on. No line says what to do: the manager decides.

    Standings

    Standings →

    The league's unit is categories, and its standings' order is the category record, as ESPN's is: the share of categories won (a tie counts half), then categories won, then fewest lost. The rows are put in that order here, from the records standings carries; five of them: yours, the two above it and the two below (the top five when you are in them).

    The category record and its share as they stand, from standings, as the ingest last stored them: a past day asked for with ?today= still shows the latest. The projected finish (the projection's mean simulated record) and the playoff odds are from projected, the answer the Standings page's Projected view draws; that projection orders its seasons and seeds their playoffs by the same category rule as the standings, so the two places are one order.
