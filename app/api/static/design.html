Scenario

the rail and the bar

The rail at the left is the live shell: your team's pages, the league's, the scenario and the switcher, with the page you are on marked in orange. At a phone's width it folds into the top bar's Menu. The bar at the top of this page is the live scenario bar, set from a real What if answer: reading it… Its toggle switches the panel below between the roster as it stands and the scenario; Reset clears it; Inspect opens where each of its three numbers came from.

A move

the day's week report

The owner's format: the man and the number, DROP, GAIN and COST as rows, the facts and Inspect. GAIN and COST are the categories whose chance of being won this week the move changes by half a point or more. Inspect opens how the number is worked out, in the drawer.

Reading the day's week report; it searches the whole wire, so a cold server takes about half a minute.

The wire

the screener table

Dense rows under a header that stays put, sortable on a click, columns you can put away (your choice is kept in this browser), a row that opens the drawer. The first table is every move the day's report considered, its nine as glyphs; the second is the day's wire with FIT, what each man is worth to this roster in a week.

Basketball, without pictures

No headshots, no silhouettes, no initials in circles. A man is his team and his position; a night is who is played and when; a week is how many games and on which days.

Not here

and why
  • Large KPI cards, giant greetings, generous whitespace. A manager comes to compare; a figure is read beside the figures it is compared with.
  • Gradients, glass, oversized rounded cards, pills everywhere. Hierarchy is borders and three surfaces; anything else is decoration.
  • Avatars, silhouettes, initials, player photographs. None, ever. PHI · SG is the mark.
  • Verdicts. A bar labels a move and hides none; the word is "worth a look". Every number's provenance is one click away, in the drawer. The other side of a deal is our estimate of their side. Nothing here acts on ESPN.
